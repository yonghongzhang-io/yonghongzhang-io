# Yonghong Zhang

PhD candidate in Economics & Business at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid.

I study causal inference, reliable AI agents, and climate-policy evaluation. My work develops benchmarks for causal research workflows and audits whether agent evaluations measure the capabilities they claim to measure.

[Homepage](https://yonghongzhang-io.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cxYmFL0AAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yonghong-zhang)

## Selected research projects

### [CausalVerify](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/causalverify)
An execution-grounded benchmark that checks whether LLM-generated causal-inference workflows execute and recover the correct numerical estimates. Under review.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07944) · [Code](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/causalverify) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yonghongzhang/causalverify)

### [ARGUS](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/ARGUS)
Evidence-grounded auditing of identification assumptions in difference-in-differences studies. Accepted, ClimateNLP Workshop @ EMNLP 2026.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30867) · [Code](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/ARGUS) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yonghongzhang/ARGUS) · [HF paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.30867)

### [ComtradeBench / OpenEnv](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/comtrade-openenv)
A trade-data API environment for evaluating agent tool use under faults and constrained budgets. The subsequent Double Measurement Confound study audits scaffold ownership, scoring validity, and reliability across seeds. Under review.

[Audit paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09218) · [Code](https://github.com/yonghongzhang-io/comtrade-openenv) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/yonghongzhang/comtrade-env)

### 🌍 GLEAM *(work in progress)*
A multilingual ESG-perception research project built around an auditable agentic LLM pipeline. The research repository is currently private while the project is under development.
